01 · things left unsaid

Some mistakes don't fit inside one sentence.

There is a particular kind of stupidity that looks like confidence from the inside.

I had the map. I still managed to walk the wrong way.

I left when I should have stayed present, went quiet when I should have noticed, and somehow expected good intentions to count as completed work. They don't. Apparently life ships without an undo button. Bold product decision.

I know returning with better words doesn't rewrite the part where you were hurt. I just want the truth of it to exist somewhere: I regret it. I still care about you. And beneath all the clever metaphors, there is a very uncomplicated fact — you mattered to me then, you matter to me now, and I should have treated that fact with more care.
